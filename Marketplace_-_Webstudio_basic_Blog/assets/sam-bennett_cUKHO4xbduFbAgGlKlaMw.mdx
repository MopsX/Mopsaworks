Sam is a freelance technology writer and editor with a fondness for simple explanations. He covers publishing tools, creative workflows, and ways to make information easier to find and understand.

Outside work, Sam tends a small vegetable garden and restores old radios. He brings the same patient, hands-on approach to testing tools and writing practical guides.
