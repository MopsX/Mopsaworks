Alex is an independent product designer who helps small teams turn rough ideas into useful digital products. He writes about prototyping, user research, and the everyday decisions behind clear interfaces.

Away from his desk, Alex collects secondhand cameras and explores unfamiliar cities on foot. His articles draw on experiments, sketches, and lessons from working with early-stage teams.
